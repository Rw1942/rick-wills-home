Email2ChatGPT.com

2026-03

Email2ChatGPT.com treats email as a universal input for AI workflows. The goal is to let people process tasks, requests, and mixed content by sending an email instead of integrating complex APIs.

This project is focused on reducing workflow friction and making AI processing available through a channel people already use every day.
